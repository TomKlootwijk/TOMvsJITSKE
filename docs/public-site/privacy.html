Privacy information

Version 1.1 · Updated 25 September 2026

This page describes how the Private Recorder Android app handles recordings and optional cloud backups. The app’s purpose is to save audio you choose to record and preserve copies in accounts you choose to connect.

Information stored on the phone

The app stores microphone audio, file sizes and hashes, recording/session identifiers, device-clock and elapsed-time values, recording configuration, and operational events such as starts, stops, and detected interruptions. These originals are kept in the app’s private storage, excluded from Android’s automatic app-data backup.

Cloud features also store account identifiers, the connected Google account email address, authorization state, destination information, settings, and backup receipts. A receipt records the files and hashes verified during a transfer. It is not continuous monitoring of the remote files.

Optional transfers to your cloud accounts

Cloud backup is disabled until you connect a provider. When connected, completed audio files and their associated metadata and event snapshots are sent directly from the phone to that provider. The app does not route recordings through a separate app-operated server.

  • Google Drive: the app requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This scope covers files created or opened through the app. Backups are stored in its Private Recorder Evidence folder in My Drive.
  • OneDrive: the app requests Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder for its application folder, plus offline_access to renew authorization for background transfers. Backups are placed in Private Recorder Evidence inside that application folder.

Google and Microsoft handle account sign-in and cloud storage under their own account settings and privacy terms. The recorder does not create public sharing links or automatically send recordings to other people. Access you grant through the cloud account’s own sharing controls is separate.

Security and encryption

Transfers use HTTPS. Saved authorization state is encrypted using a device-bound Android Keystore key. Provider bearer credentials are not forwarded to redirected download hosts. Access tokens and authorization state are not included in audio exports.

The app does not add end-to-end or password encryption to recordings. Audio and metadata are uploaded as ordinary readable files over HTTPS, and exported ZIP files are not password-encrypted by the app. Protect the phone, connected accounts, and any exported copies.

Before accepting an upload, the app downloads and hashes the remote content. This verifies matching bytes at that time; it does not protect a copy from later deletion or changes made outside the app.

Retention, deletion, and disconnecting

  • The app does not automatically delete old phone recordings after backup. They continue to use phone storage.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes its private phone archive and saved app state. Preserve copies first if you need to keep them.
  • Disconnecting a cloud provider stops future scheduled backups and removes its authorization state saved by this app. A request already sent may have reached the provider. Disconnecting leaves existing phone recordings and cloud copies intact.
  • Delete unwanted cloud files using that provider’s controls. Deleting a cloud file does not delete its phone original. Historical receipts do not guarantee that a deleted or changed cloud file will be detected or uploaded again.
  • You can additionally remove the app’s account authorization in the relevant provider’s account settings. Copies you export or share separately must be managed at their respective destinations.

Advertising and analytics

The app has no advertising or app analytics feature. It does not sell recordings or use them for advertising. Account sign-in and cloud requests still involve the selected provider, and the operating system or provider may process service information under its own policies.

These information pages contain no forms, scripts, advertising, or tracking pixels.
