Android audio recording

Your recordings.
Your chosen accounts.

Private Recorder saves microphone audio on your Android phone. Optional cloud connections copy finished recordings directly to dedicated folders in your Google Drive and OneDrive accounts.

You start and stop recording in the app. Android’s recording notification and microphone indicator remain visible, including when recording continues with the screen off.

How it works

  • Phone originals: audio is saved in approximately five-minute AAC segments, with timestamps, recording events, and integrity hashes. Recording does not need an Internet connection.
  • Optional account backups: connect Google Drive, OneDrive, or both. Each provider has its own queue and dedicated destination. Pending files stay on the phone when a connection is unavailable.
  • Transfer verification: the app downloads uploaded files and compares their SHA-256 hashes before recording a successful backup receipt.
  • Your controls: choose whether backups may use mobile data, disconnect a provider, or export a ZIP of completed recordings. The app does not automatically delete originals.

Account access

Google Drive uses drive.file access for files created or opened through this app. OneDrive uses Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder access for its application folder. Cloud connections are disabled until you authorize them in the app.

Know the limits

Continuous recording and immediate backup are not guaranteed. Android, other microphone apps, calls, power loss, full storage, or network problems can interrupt recording or delay transfers. Test the actual phone and check the audio and saved copies regularly.

A successful hash comparison records matching bytes at one moment. It does not prove continuous capture, speaker identity, accurate real-world timestamps, or the continuing availability of a cloud copy.

Read the privacy information and personal-use information and operating limits before connecting accounts.
